What Wordelate does

Wordelate is a free browser game. Every day there is one puzzle with a start word and a goal word. You add or subtract words to land on the goal, and the game shows your path as a graph you can look around.

1

Start from a word

Each day’s puzzle gives you a start word and a goal word to reach.

2

Add and subtract words

Type a word and add it or subtract it. Each move shifts your meaning toward, or away from, the goal.

3

Reach the goal

Land on the goal word to win. Fewer moves score more points. You have up to 16 moves.

Points, streaks and rewards

Earn points for each win, keep a daily streak going and unlock new looks as you play.

Daily leaderboard

See how your score ranks against everyone who played today. Putting a name on the board is optional.

Past days and sharing

Practice earlier puzzles without changing their boards, and share your result with friends.

Optional account

Log in with Google to keep your scores and streak on every device and compare with friends by private link.

Why Wordelate asks for your data

You can play without logging in or sharing anything. If you choose “Log in with Google”, Wordelate asks Google for only your basic profile: the openid, email and profile scopes. Wordelate does not ask for access to your Gmail, Drive, contacts, calendar or any other Google service.

What we receiveWhat we use it for
Google account ID To recognize you when you come back, so your account is tied to you and not to a device.
Email address To identify your account. It is shown only to you on your account page, never to other players or friends.
Name To set your first username. You can change your username at any time. Friends and leaderboards see your username, not your full name.
Profile picture address To show your picture next to your name on the leaderboard and to friends. We keep only the web address, never the image, and you can turn the picture off at any time.
  • Why log in at all: to save your scores and streak to your account, sync them across devices, and compare with friends who share a private link with you.
  • Your game data (scores, points, streaks, friend connections) is stored with your account for those same features, and backed up weekly.
  • You are in control: log out, log out everywhere, or delete your account and its saved scores from your account page.
  • Wordelate’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
